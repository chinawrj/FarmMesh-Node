# Independent power review — A0 draft

Date: 2026-09-23. Scope: `design-manifest.json`, the power section and power-related connections in `scripts/build_design.py`, and the assigned KiCad footprints. This is a static schematic review; no PCB, simulation, or hardware measurements were performed.

The corrections below are present in both the generator and regenerated manifest. No remaining static connection errors were found within this review scope. Component selection and bench validation remain open as listed below.

## Verified connections and corrections

- **LTC3119IFE#PBF:** FE pin mapping, all power/ground pins, exposed pad, 4.7 µH SW1–SW2 inductor, and both 100 nF bootstrap capacitors match the official reference. The assigned TSSOP-28 footprint has the required 3.05 × 7.56 mm exposed pad. See the [LTC3119 datasheet](https://www.analog.com/media/en/technical-documentation/data-sheets/3119fb.pdf), pp. 2, 12, 28 and 34.
- **Control network:** 316 kΩ/100 kΩ feedback gives 3.307 V nominal; 162 kΩ RT gives approximately 500 kHz; VC has 78.7 kΩ and 820 pF in series to ground. MPPC/SVCC connect to VCC, VCC has 4.7 µF bypass, and PWM/SYNC is grounded. These use TA06 as the starting circuit. PGOOD has a 100 kΩ pull-up and connects only to RP2350 GPIO36. The [PMEG2010EA](https://assets.nexperia.com/documents/data-sheet/PMEG2010EA.pdf) has pin 2/anode at 3V3 and pin 1/cathode at VCC.
- **Reverse battery protection:** the fused battery connects to Q1 drain; source connects to the converter; gate has 100 kΩ to ground. The Q1 symbol now uses pad 5 for physical drain pins 5–8 and exposed drain, matching the stock footprint's merged copper pad. See [DMP2008UFG](https://www.diodes.com/datasheet/download/DMP2008UFG.pdf). This circuit does not provide reverse-current isolation from an externally powered 3V3 rail.
- **UVLO:** 174 kΩ/100 kΩ and 470 pF give nominal 3.30 V start and 3.01 V stop thresholds; tolerance and input sag apply. The OFF jumper grounds RUN. UVLO stopping switching does not guarantee full shutdown: the LTC3119 requires RUN below 0.3 V for its minimum shutdown-current state. An external protected cell/pack remains necessary; no charger or raw PV input is implemented.
- **C7 footprint and polarity corrected:** 6SVPE220M is Panasonic case C6, diameter 6.3 mm and nominal height 5.9 mm. `CP_Elec_6.3x5.9` matches the manufacturer's land pattern: 2.1 mm inner gap, 9.1 mm overall length, 1.6 mm pad width. C6/C7/C203/C303/C403 now use polarized symbols with pin 1 positive and pin 2 grounded. Sources: [6SVPE220M specifications](https://industrial.panasonic.com/sa/products/pt/os-con/models/6SVPE220M), [SVPE dimensions](https://industrial.panasonic.com/cdbs/www-data/pdf/AAB8000/AAB8000C179.pdf), [recommended lands](https://industrial.panasonic.com/cdbs/www-data/pdf/AAB8000/AAB8000COL10.pdf).
- **Unpowered ADC backfeed paths removed:** R106/R107/C125 battery monitoring is deferred and GPIO40 is NC. USB VBUS sensing was moved from ADC GPIO41 to fault-tolerant GPIO37, retaining the divider. The divided USB voltage stays below the FT pin's 3.63 V unpowered limit for normal USB VBUS. ADC-capable pins are not covered by that FT allowance. See [RP2350 datasheet](https://datasheets.raspberrypi.com/rp2350/rp2350-datasheet.pdf), sections 12.4, 14.8.2 and 14.9.1.

## Budget and remaining validation

The 2.2 A rail budget includes the three modules' combined minimum 1.8 A supply capability plus RP2350 and margin; it is not a measured consumption figure. The module requirement is in [ESP32-C5-WROOM-1/1U datasheet](https://www.espressif.com/sites/default/files/documentation/esp32-c5-wroom-1_wroom-1u_datasheet_en.pdf), v1.3, table 6-2. The 3 A figure is a transient validation target, not a guaranteed continuous rating.

Using an explicitly assumed 80% efficiency, `Iin ≈ 3.3 × Iout / (Vin × 0.80)` gives about 3.03 A input for 2.2 A output, or 4.13 A for 3 A output, at 3.0 V at the converter. Cell/BMS, fuse, connector and wiring drops must be included. Converter loss under this assumption is about 1.82 W at 2.2 A output; this is a thermal budget, not an efficiency measurement.

- Confirm 2.2 A operation and the intended 3 A pulse profile across 3.0–4.2 V, temperature, startup and simultaneous radio activity. Measure module-pin voltage, ripple, overshoot, input sag, fuse behavior, and IC/inductor/MOSFET temperature.
- Verify compensation and transient response with the actual output capacitors, including all three local 100 µF reservoirs. TA06 compensation alone does not validate the larger distributed capacitance. C7 is 220 µF nominal ±20%, not a guaranteed 220 µF minimum.
- Complete exact MPN, capacitance under DC bias, ESR/ripple-current and footprint selection for the provisional bulk and ceramic capacitors. Only C7's specific polymer MPN/footprint was closed by this review.
- [XAL7070-472MEC](https://www.coilcraft.com/getmedia/1ba55433-bcc8-4838-9b21-382f497e12e0/xal7070.pdf) is 4.7 µH ±20%, DCR about 14.3 mΩ maximum, with 15.2 A saturation current defined at 25°C and a typical 30% inductance drop. Check hot inductance and temperature rise. LTC3119 average-inductor and peak current limits are not output-current ratings.
- Validate UVLO threshold spread, restart hysteresis and residual battery drain. Verify the selected protected pack's cutoff, current capability and fault behavior. Keep externally powered debugger/UART supply outputs disconnected from the reference-only 3V3 headers.
- PCB hot loops, ground returns, exposed-pad cooling, RF interference and production assembly are outside this schematic-only review.
